Stop guessing the pattern. Ground it.

Paste a problem statement and get back the pattern it actually belongs to — retrieved from a curated reference library and checked by an LLM against that material before it commits to an answer, instead of a confident guess pulled from thin air.

Analyze a problem →

How it works

  1. Embed — your problem statement becomes a vector.
  2. Retrieve — the closest-matching patterns come back from the reference library, ranked by similarity.
  3. Judge — an LLM checks the fit against the retrieved material and explains it, or says plainly when nothing matches.
